THE STARS

Luminous spheres of plasma held together by gravity. These cosmic furnaces create the elements that form planets, oceans, life, and everything humanity has ever touched.

Temperature

Millions of degrees inside stellar cores.

Main Element

Hydrogen fuels nuclear fusion reactions.

Luminosity

Stars radiate enormous amounts of energy.

Lifespan

From millions to trillions of years.

Observable Stars

Scientists estimate there are septillions of stars across the observable universe.

Closest Star

The Sun is the closest star to Earth and the source of nearly all energy on our planet.

Star Types

Stars exist as red dwarfs, white dwarfs, giants, supergiants, neutron stars and more.

Stellar Birth

Stars are born inside giant molecular clouds when gravity collapses dense regions of gas.
