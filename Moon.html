Nearest Celestial Neighbor

THE MOON

Earth's natural satellite and humanity's first destination beyond our planet. The Moon shapes tides, stabilizes Earth's rotation, and serves as a stepping stone toward future space exploration.

Orbital Period

27.3 Days

Distance

384,400 km

Gravity

16.6% Earth

Surface

Regolith Craters

Apollo Missions

Six Apollo missions successfully landed humans on the lunar surface between 1969 and 1972.

Lunar Origin

Most scientists believe the Moon formed after a giant collision between Earth and a Mars-sized object.

Future Bases

Space agencies plan permanent lunar stations to support scientific research and deep-space missions.
